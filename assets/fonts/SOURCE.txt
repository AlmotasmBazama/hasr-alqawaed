Cairo font source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cairo
Downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/cairo/Cairo%5Bslnt%2Cwght%5D.ttf
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/cairo/OFL.txt

Cairo-Regular.ttf and Cairo-Bold.ttf are static instances of this upstream
variable font, instantiated using fontTools at wght=400/700 and slnt=0.
The original Cairo-Variable.ttf and SIL Open Font License are retained.
Both static fonts are embedded locally in the app and PDF; no runtime downloads.
